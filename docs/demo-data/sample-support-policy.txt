Little Birdies Store Support Policy

Shipping:
Standard shipping usually takes 2 to 3 business days after an order is confirmed. Same-week delivery may be available for selected items in supported cities.

Returns:
Customers can request a return within 30 days of delivery when the item is unused and in original packaging. Refunds are reviewed by a support agent when an order is late, damaged, or missing.

Warranty:
Wireless headphones include a 12-month warranty for manufacturing defects. Warranty claims require the order number and a short description of the issue.

Invoices:
Customers can request another invoice copy by providing the order number and the email address used at checkout.

Escalation:
Complaints about refunds, broken products, late delivery, cancellation, or angry customer messages should be handed off to a human support agent.
