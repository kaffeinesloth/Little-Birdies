- Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery.
- Size, color, or model exchanges are free when the item does not fit or meet expectations.
- Manufacturing defects such as loose seams, faulty zippers, or sole defects qualify for an immediate one-for-one replacement during the first seven days, with all shipping paid by the store.
- Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and have their original tags attached.
- Athletic shoes and backpacks have a six-month warranty for adhesive, stitching, and zipper defects.
- Equipment, insulated bottles, and yoga mats have a 12-month technical warranty.
